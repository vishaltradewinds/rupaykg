# RupayKg Product Specification

## Vision
Build a nationwide operating platform connecting physical waste/resource activity to trustworthy measurement, evidence, MRV, carbon, EPR, credentials, registry, settlement and reporting.

## Six pillars
1. Waste & Resource Recovery
2. Sovereign Digital MRV
3. Carbon Accounting
4. EPR Compliance
5. Enterprise ESG Reporting
6. AI-Driven Operational Intelligence

## Authoritative lifecycle
GENERATION -> AGGREGATION -> MEASUREMENT -> TRANSPORT -> PROCESSING -> EVIDENCE -> VERIFICATION -> VALUE CALCULATION -> CERTIFICATION/ISSUANCE -> REGISTRY -> TRANSFER/RETIREMENT -> SETTLEMENT -> REPORTING

## Trust rule
RupayKg must not claim that an activity, measurement, compliance outcome, credential, registry transition or payment occurred unless the required authoritative persistence, evidence and verification conditions exist.

## Operating modes
- Urban: ULB, ward, household/commercial/bulk generator, collection, transfer, MRF and processor flows.
- Rural: Gram Panchayat, village/cluster, household/farmer/SHG, local aggregation and regional processing flows.
- Industrial: generators, processors, authorized recyclers, EPR and enterprise reporting.

## Evidence classes
Evidence may include GPS, timestamps, weighbridge records, photographs, documents, device readings, facility records, chain-of-custody events, verifier decisions and external authority confirmations. Evidence must be linked to the event it supports.

## Value realization
Environmental and financial value is downstream of verified physical activity. Settlement is a governed workflow and may integrate automated payment or smart-contract execution where explicitly configured and reconciled.
