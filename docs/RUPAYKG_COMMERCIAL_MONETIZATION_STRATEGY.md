# RupayKg Commercial Monetization — Product, Services and DPG Sourcing Strategy

**Effective:** 2026-11-07  
**Authority:** Commercial strategy proposal  
**Core rule:** Monetization first; open-source/DPG adoption only where it improves commercial value.

## 1. Positioning

RupayKg is a proprietary commercial operating platform for environmental resource flows, compliance operations and digital MRV.

It sells software, operational efficiency, traceability, integration, reporting, managed services and support. It does not claim to be a public regulator, statutory approval body, accredited verifier or official carbon-credit issuer.

DPGs are optional technology inputs. Their use must reduce cost, accelerate a customer deliverable, expand an integration channel or create a sellable service. A DPG listing does not itself produce revenue, exclusivity or customer demand.

## 2. Product packages

| Package | Customer | Revenue model |
|---|---|---|
| Resource Flow Operations | ULBs, waste generators, collectors, processors and recyclers | Annual organisation/site subscription; onboarding and support |
| Compliance Operations | Bulk waste generators, producers/brand owners, enterprises and public bodies, subject to applicability | Annual module subscription; reporting-cycle and facility bands |
| Digital MRV Operations | Waste, landfill, biomass and circular-economy project developers | Project/site onboarding plus recurring monitoring and evidence-management subscription |
| Enterprise / Government | Multi-site organisations, states, ULBs and PSUs | Annual contract, implementation, SLA and managed operations |
| API / Integration | Software providers, system integrators, large customers and public agencies | Setup, integration maintenance, usage bands and support |
| Managed Data & Reporting | Customers needing data curation, reconciliation, audit packages or public dataset publication | Monthly/annual service scope |
| Partner / OEM | Device, weighing, laboratory, audit and technology partners | Integration, reseller/OEM or support contracts |

## 3. Revenue principles

- Price by measurable service value, facility/site count, organisation, reporting period, API tier, project workload and SLA.
- Keep one-time implementation revenue separate from recurring subscription and managed-service revenue.
- Do not invent prices before customer, budget, competitor and cost-to-serve validation.
- Do not depend on carbon-credit success fees as the primary business model.
- Never charge for favourable verification, statutory approval, certificate issuance or regulator acceptance.
- Preserve data export, customer exit, confidentiality and lawful access. Customer data is not an asset for resale.
- Clearly price hosting, data volume, hardware, travel, third-party providers, support and taxes.

## 4. Defensible commercial assets

Keep proprietary by default:
- production platform source and deployment automation;
- tenant, identity, organisation, geography and access control plane;
- statutory applicability and compliance workflow implementation;
- methodology adapters, calculation orchestration and audit trace implementation;
- evidence vault, quality gates and verification workflow;
- registry, credential, transfer, retirement and settlement orchestration;
- analytics, commercial reporting and customer-specific configuration;
- device, trust-provider and external-system adapters;
- deployment, monitoring, backup, support and managed-operation playbooks;
- pricing, packaging, billing and partner-management services.

This is an IP boundary policy, not a legal conclusion that all listed assets are owned by RupayKg. Third-party data, public standards, government publications, open-source dependencies and customer-owned information remain governed by their own rights and contracts.

## 5. What to publish openly

Open selectively, after legal and security review:
- API specifications and integration contracts;
- neutral data schemas and data dictionaries;
- interoperability mappings;
- non-personal synthetic examples and conformance fixtures;
- supported connector documentation;
- selected implementation guides that do not disclose trade secrets or security-sensitive internals.

An open specification is not an open-source implementation. A published API does not require publishing the private implementation that serves it.

## 6. DPG/DPGA policy

1. Do not make DPG recognition the primary commercial objective.
2. Do not add an open-source licence or remove package-private settings in the commercial repository without explicit rights-holder approval.
3. Do not extract and publish the existing domain/carbon packages wholesale.
4. Use selected recognised DPGs as separately governed components or services where they solve a customer need.
5. Keep their licences, notices, security, hosting, support and maintenance obligations explicit.
6. Treat DPGA recognition as optional; nominate only a separately scoped product if the strategic benefit outweighs loss of exclusivity and all DPGA criteria are evidenced.
7. Do not imply DPGA partnership or government endorsement through use of a listed DPG.

## 7. Monetizable DPG integration services

- OpenFn connector implementation and managed workflow operations.
- ODK partner field-data onboarding and controlled ingestion.
- Sunbird RC or CREDEBL configuration and credential interoperability, if a real use case justifies one.
- CKAN-based managed open environmental data portals, with separate deployment and AGPL compliance.
- X-Road integration advisory and deployment support only for institutional/government federation programmes.
- Data quality, transformation, reconciliation and source-to-report assurance.

RupayKg charges for its own engineering, deployment, configuration, hosting, integration, support and service commitments—not for the public DPGA listing or a free right to use a DPG.

## 8. Commercial execution metrics

Track:
- contracted ARR and recurring revenue mix;
- qualified pipeline, conversion, average contract value and sales cycle;
- gross margin by SaaS, implementation and managed service;
- onboarding cost and time-to-value;
- active paying organisations and facilities;
- renewals, churn and net revenue retention;
- cost to serve per site, data volume and reporting cycle;
- integration revenue and partner-sourced pipeline;
- support cost, uptime, incident response and cash collection.

## 9. Immediate management and engineering decisions

- Keep vishaltradewinds/rupaykg main authoritative.
- Keep DPG Registry sourcing and commercial strategy in a separate documentation/decision track.
- Use a cached, attributed DPGA Registry API snapshot only for a future curated integration catalogue; no runtime dependency.
- Prioritise an OpenFn technical/licence assessment and an ODK import contract as discovery tasks, not production approvals.
- Do not replace existing RupayKg registries, field app, evidence store, identity or MRV workflows without a validated customer requirement and controlled migration plan.
- Require a named buyer/use case, budget owner, measurable value and cost-to-serve before production integration.
- Keep production, statutory, certification, field and TRL gates unchanged.

## 10. Commercial decision

**RupayKg remains a proprietary commercial product.** The business monetises trusted operations, integration and managed services around interoperable digital ecosystems. Open standards help distribution; carefully selected DPGs reduce duplication; proprietary workflows, service quality, operational evidence and customer relationships sustain the business.

**Status:** Internal strategy proposal; not a price list, signed customer contract, revenue forecast or legal opinion.
