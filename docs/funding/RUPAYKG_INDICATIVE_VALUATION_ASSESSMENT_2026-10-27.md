## 4. Valuation method

Because revenue and customer metrics are not established, do not apply ARR, revenue, GMV or EBITDA multiples. Use a **pre-revenue scorecard / risk-adjusted milestone method**, cross-checked against broad Indian pre-seed reference bands.

The online practitioner benchmark reviewed for 2026 gives a broad ₹3–10 crore pre-seed band, but explicitly characterises it as an indicative reference class rather than a measured median or a valuation of RupayKg. Source: https://virtualauditor.in/learn/india-startup-valuation-benchmarks-2026/. Other published startup guides show similarly broad ranges and stress product evidence, team, IP, customer pull and comparable transactions. These secondary sources are directional only; actual comparable financing data has not been independently assembled for this assessment.

## 7. Milestones that could increase value

Reassess valuation only when evidence is available for each milestone:

1. **Legal/IP gate:** Confirm entity, founder shareholding, repository ownership, contributor assignments, open-source licence compliance, third-party IP and data rights.
2. **Reproducible build gate:** Record the exact commit SHA, clean install/build/test outputs and dependency/security results for the candidate release.
3. **Live-operability gate:** Demonstrate an authorised staging deployment with correct database identity, migrations, audit-chain checks, least privilege, backup and isolated restore evidence.
4. **External integration gate:** Independently verify any claimed Guardian/Hedera/registry integration; distinguish local workflow from external acceptance and official registry issuance.
5. **Customer validation gate:** Obtain permissioned buyer interviews, letters of intent, design-partner agreements or contracts; distinguish unpaid interest from signed revenue.
6. **Impact/MRV gate:** Define baseline, boundary, methodology, data lineage, uncertainty and independent verification. Do not claim carbon credits or emissions reductions without required evidence.
7. **Commercial gate:** Establish pricing, cost to serve, gross margin, sales cycle, deployment effort, retention and recurring revenue from records.
8. **Independent review gate:** Complete a technical/security review and an independent valuation review if required for the transaction.

Use the same milestone evidence to update the valuation range, pitch deck and funding applications. Do not raise the valuation merely because more code or documentation has been added.

## 9. Shakti Standards valuation controls

- **Reality and context:** distinguish repository work, runnable prototype, tested MVP, live deployment and commercial operations.
- **Evidence:** every investor-facing statement must map to an artefact, owner, date and reviewer.
- **Compliance:** do not represent RupayKg as a government-authorised registry, accredited verifier or carbon-credit issuer without formal proof.
- **Feasibility:** valuation is not a substitute for a costed budget or validated business model.
- **Testing and approval:** CI results and internal audits must be labelled with exact commit and scope; independent review is separate.
- **Deployment and operation:** production readiness remains a separate gate and is not inferred from code completeness.
- **Measurement and assurance:** update value only when product, customer, financial and impact metrics are evidenced.
- **TRL:** assess components individually and do not assign a higher overall TRL without qualifying operating evidence.
