## Findings that affect execution

### MP Startup Seed Fund Assistance (MPSSFA)
- The MP Startup Policy 2025 states that selected startups may receive grant assistance up to ₹30 lakh through empanelled incubators.
- The official SOP describes a sequence: incubators are empanelled; eligible startups apply through the MP Startup portal; applications are mapped to empanelled incubators; incubator evaluation and state due diligence follow; approval is made by the competent authority; sanctioned amounts are disbursed by MPLUN, potentially in tranches.
- The public incubator directory is not, by itself, proof that a listed incubator is empanelled for MPSSFA. Confirm the active MPSSFA empanelment list directly with the Madhya Pradesh Startup Centre.
- The portal/policy presence does not establish that a new application window is open today. Ask for written/current confirmation before preparing a submission around a deadline.
- Grant support is conditional, not guaranteed. Allowed uses include manpower, professional services, consumables, hardware/software/raw material, outsourced manufacturing, product testing, market research, market entry, commercialisation and scale-up; construction, land/property and fixed-capital investment are excluded under the published policy.

### DPIIT recognition
Official Startup India guidance states eligible entities include private limited companies, registered partnership firms, LLPs and cooperative societies, with innovation/scalability criteria and turnover/age limits. Current published normal-startup threshold is turnover below ₹200 crore and up to 10 years from incorporation; DeepTech recognition criteria are different. Check the current portal because the notification and criteria can change.
- Application is through the National Single Window System (NSWS), by the startup itself.
- Startup India says recognition application is free and warns against unauthorised agents.
- RupayKg's legal entity and recognition status remain unverified. Do not start or certify an application using assumed entity details.

### NSRCEL Velocity
- The official page currently says applications are open.
- Eligibility includes DPIIT registration, a market-ready MVP, revenue generation, and an existing customer base.
- A select subset of cohort startups may receive grants up to ₹30 lakh; this is not guaranteed for all accepted startups.
- Because current RupayKg evidence does not establish revenue and existing customers, treat this as CONDITIONAL and do not misstate traction. If criteria are not met, ask the programme whether there is a future-stage route or wait until evidence exists.
