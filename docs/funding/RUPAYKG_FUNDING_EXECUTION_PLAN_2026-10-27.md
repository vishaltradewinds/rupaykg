# RupayKg Funding Execution Plan — India (27 October 2026)

## Purpose
Build an evidence-led funding pipeline for RupayKg, positioned as ClimateTech / CarbonTech / digital Measurement, Reporting and Verification (dMRV) infrastructure for waste and circular-economy workflows.

This plan is an execution tracker, not a claim of eligibility, application submission, award, government endorsement, or regulatory certification. Programme status must be reconfirmed on the official page before submission.

## Current evidence posture
- Product stage: treat as under development / not yet independently validated in a live operating environment until current deployment and test evidence is assembled.
- DPIIT recognition: UNVERIFIED — obtain certificate or confirm application status.
- Incorporation, date, registered office, MP GST registration and ownership: UNVERIFIED — obtain official records.
- Revenue and customers: UNVERIFIED — do not claim revenue, paying customers, or traction without records.
- TRL: NOT YET ESTABLISHED for the product as a whole. Assess each component separately under Shakti Standards and attach evidence.
- Environmental outcomes: not independently verified. Do not claim quantified emission reductions or carbon credits without an approved methodology and verifiable evidence.
- Regulatory posture: describe RupayKg as workflow, evidence and dMRV-support infrastructure. Do not claim it issues government-recognised carbon credits or replaces authorised verification/registry functions.

## Immediate work batches

### Batch 1 — Legal eligibility (P0)
Collect and verify:
- Certificate of Incorporation / legal entity name / CIN or LLPIN.
- Incorporation date and registered office address.
- DPIIT recognition certificate and number, or actual application status.
- MP GST registration, where applicable.
- Founder/shareholding details and any category-based eligibility evidence, if applicable.
- Company PAN and bank details only through official application channels; never place sensitive numbers in this public repository.
- Prior grants, government assistance, investment and overlapping expense declarations.

Exit gate: eligibility matrix completed with documentary evidence; unknowns remain explicitly marked.

### Batch 2 — Product and TRL evidence (P0)
- Current repository commit, build/test results, deployment status and screenshots/logs that can safely be shared.
- Feature matrix: implemented / tested / deployed / planned.
- Component-level TRL assessment with evidence and gaps.
- Security and privacy posture, data lineage, audit trails, human approval gates and failure handling.
- Clearly label integrations not yet verified in live systems (for example, external registry writes, cryptographic credential signing, field hardware connectivity or production rate limiting if still outstanding).
- No overall TRL 5/6 claim without qualifying evidence.

Exit gate: evidence-backed product brief and TRL assessment reviewed under Shakti Standards.

### Batch 3 — Commercial and impact evidence (P1)
- Customer discovery records and letters of interest / pilot intent, with permission.
- Revenue evidence only if actual invoices/bank/accounting records exist.
- Target customer and buyer map; pricing hypotheses clearly marked as assumptions.
- Impact measurement plan: data source, baseline, methodology, uncertainty, verification and auditability.
- Explain the buyer's specific problem and why existing spreadsheets or software do not adequately solve it.

Exit gate: claims substantiated or removed.

### Batch 4 — Application pack (P1)
Prepare modular documents:
1. One-page company profile.
2. 10-slide investor/incubator pitch deck.
3. Technical architecture and product roadmap.
4. Component-level TRL and validation plan.
5. Market/customer and commercial model.
6. Funding request budget with milestone-linked use of funds.
7. Compliance and risk register.
8. Founder/team profiles and legal documents.
9. Impact measurement and verification plan.
10. Data room index.

No requested funding amount is fixed until a bottom-up budget is completed. Do not assume grants can be combined or the same cost claimed twice.

## Draft positioning
**RupayKg is a ClimateTech and CarbonTech platform building digital MRV and environmental compliance workflows for waste and circular-economy value chains.** It is designed to support evidence capture, traceability, reporting, and verification workflows. Any claim about regulatory recognition, credit issuance, verified reductions, production operation, or nationwide deployment must be backed by evidence and appropriate authorisation.

## Funding request methodology
Build the request from costed deliverables rather than a round number:
- Product engineering and testing.
- Independent technical/security validation.
- Environmental methodology and compliance expertise.
- Customer validation and eligible pilot work.
- Necessary field integration, only where justified.
- Commercialisation and market access.
- Reporting, audit and contingency within scheme rules.

For each line, specify amount, quotation/basis, timeline, output, acceptance test, evidence artefact, and whether the cost is eligible under the target programme.

## Operating rules (Shakti Standards)
1. No unsupported claims.
2. Verify live programme status and eligibility from official sources.
3. Separate planned, implemented, tested, deployed, independently validated, and certified capabilities.
4. Keep programme-specific criteria and application deadlines current.
5. No production certification, external regulatory representation, or grant submission is implied by this plan.
6. Record reviewer, date, source, evidence and unresolved risks for each gate.
