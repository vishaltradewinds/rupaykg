## 2. Executive summary
RupayKg is a ClimateTech / CarbonTech platform being developed to support digital environmental compliance, waste traceability and digital Measurement, Reporting and Verification (dMRV) workflows for waste and circular-economy value chains. The intended product connects evidence capture, traceable records, reporting workflows and verification support so organisations can manage environmental data more consistently and make records easier to review.

Waste and circular-economy workflows can involve fragmented records across generators, processors, transporters, weighbridges, recyclers, auditors and reporting systems. RupayKg aims to provide:

- Digital workflows for environmental reporting and compliance support.
