## Status definitions
- OPEN: work can proceed.
- BLOCKED: required fact/evidence is missing.
- CONDITIONAL: proceed only if stated conditions are evidenced.
- HOLD: do not apply until a new official call or change in eligibility.
- COMPLETE: evidence has been checked and recorded.

## First actions
1. Founders supply legal-entity and DPIIT status evidence.
2. Confirm MP GST registration and state policy eligibility.
3. Assemble current product evidence and component-level TRL.
4. Contact the MP Startup Center / relevant empanelled incubator to confirm the current seed-fund application route.
5. Only then prepare and submit a complete eligible application.
