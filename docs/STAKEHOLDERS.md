# Stakeholders and Roles

The platform supports, but is not limited to, these participant classes:

- Citizens, households and waste generators
- Commercial and bulk waste generators
- Industries and rural enterprises
- Farmers and self-help groups
- Waste pickers and collection workers
- Aggregators and transporters
- MRFs and recycling/processing facilities
- Composting, biogas and treatment facilities
- Waste-to-energy operators
- Producers, brand owners and importers
- EPR obligated entities
- ULBs and Panchayati Raj Institutions
- District/state authorities and regulators
- Authorized recyclers/processors
- Auditors and MRV verifiers
- Carbon/environmental-asset participants
- Registry/depository participants
- Financial and settlement participants
- NGOs and community organizations
- Platform administrators and authorized service providers

Roles must be assigned through organization-scoped permissions and geography scopes. A user must never gain authority merely because a UI route exists.

High-risk actions such as verification, credential issuance, registry transfer/retirement, obligation approval and settlement completion require explicit authorization and auditable decisions.
