# Carbon Accounting

Carbon calculations are derived from verified activity and measurement records. Each calculation stores the methodology identifier, version, inputs, factors, units, calculation timestamp and result.

The platform may support CCTS/BEE-aligned workflows and other applicable methodologies, but it must not claim regulatory compliance or credit issuance merely because a calculation completed.

Project documentation should be generated from persisted project facts and methodology rules, with explicit validation/verification status.

Calculation states:
`DRAFT -> CALCULATED -> REVIEWED -> APPROVED` or `REJECTED`.

A carbon result cannot become an issued environmental asset without the configured verification and issuance conditions.
